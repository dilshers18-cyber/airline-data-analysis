The question

Where are delays concentrated, which months show higher historical rates, and how are delay minutes attributed? This academic portfolio example examines a supplied BTS-attributed extract covering June 2003 through January 2025.

The approach

Audit 398,233 input records, exclude 950 rows with missing numeric data and two with negative measures, calculate flight-weighted rates, and compare carriers and airports within the latest complete year (2024). Preserve the input checksum and each cleaning decision.

Three findings

  • 19.23% of reported flights in retained records had delayed arrivals; average delay among delayed arrivals was 59.93 minutes.
  • Late aircraft contributed 38.43% of attributed delay minutes, followed by carrier causes at 31.42%.
  • Across full years 2004-2024, June's pooled delay rate was 23.36%, compared with September's 15.00%.

Quality checks and practical use

All 17 pandas tests passed. An independent Python csv/math calculation checked all six tables and 713 exported values. This workflow demonstrates cleaning, analysis, chart creation and reproducible reporting.

Scope and limitations

Monthly aggregates are not individual flight records. Complete-case exclusions may bias results. Rankings are not route-adjusted, and coverage and carrier definitions can change. January 2025 is not a complete year. Extreme weather is not all weather impact. Findings describe this historical extract, not current airline performance.
